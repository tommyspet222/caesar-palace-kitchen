Our story

A personal touch.
A memorable table.

Caesar Palace Kitchen brings fine-dining inspiration to a personal, made-to-order experience. We believe a memorable meal begins with considered ingredients, careful preparation and the joy of sharing good food.

Our online kitchen is based in Lagos, Nigeria. We welcome enquiries for meals, pastry, private-chef experiences and event catering. There is no walk-in restaurant; each request is discussed directly before anything is confirmed.
